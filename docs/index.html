Engineering Precision

Designed for creators,
built for performance.

Traditional phone webcam tools force cloud relays, driver installations, subscriptions, and battery-draining apps. FloatCam takes a fundamentally simpler approach.

Pure Local Wi-Fi WebRTC

Your video stream never touches the public internet. Signaling happens over local WebSockets, and media streams directly between your phone and computer over LAN peer-to-peer connection with hardware H.264 acceleration.

SUB-SECOND LATENCY · ZERO TELEMETRY

Session-Token Isolation

FloatCam generates an ephemeral 8-character cryptographic token per session. Other devices on the same Wi-Fi cannot hijack or listen to your stream.

CRYPTOGRAPHIC PRIVACY

Zero Mobile Apps Needed

Open your default camera app, scan the dashboard QR code, and tap Start. Works natively in iOS Safari and Android Chrome via standard HTML5 MediaDevices.

NO APP STORE INSTALLATION

Dynamic Port Selection

Never worry about "Port 9211 in use". FloatCam automatically probes and secures an unoccupied port between 9211 and 9299 on your computer.

COLLISION-FREE DAEMON

Integrated Camera Fallback

Phone battery running low? Switch instantly to your built-in Mac FaceTime or Windows integrated webcam with one click in the tray menu.
