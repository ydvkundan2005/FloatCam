Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

FloatCam is built for creators, developers, and anyone who needs a better webcam without compromise.

📱
Wireless Phone Camera

Stream from iPhone (Safari) or Android (Chrome) over local Wi-Fi. No USB, no cables, no apps to install on your phone.

⚡
Sub-Second Latency

Direct P2P WebRTC with hardware acceleration. Smooth, real-time preview without the lag of cloud-based solutions.

🛡️
100% Private & Local

Zero cloud relays. Zero accounts. Zero telemetry. Every video packet stays on your local network — always.

🎨
10 Geometric Shapes

Circle, Squircle, Hexagon, Heart, Star, Diamond, Octagon, Rounded Rectangle, Square, and 16:9 Rectangle.

🔍
Live Zoom & Opacity

Real-time digital zoom slider (1.0×–3.0×) and opacity control (10%–100%). Drag corners to resize instantly.

📶
QR Code Connection

No app needed. Point your phone's default camera at the QR code and you're connected in seconds.

📍
Corner Snapping

One-click snap to any corner — top-left, top-right, bottom-left, or bottom-right. Perfect for any layout.

💻
Built-in Mac Webcam

Toggle to your FaceTime or integrated camera anytime without closing FloatCam.

🖥️
Menu Bar & System Tray

Runs as a lightweight background utility on macOS (LSUIElement) and Windows System Tray.

How It Works

Up and running in 30 seconds.

No configuration. No accounts. No cloud. Just your phone and your Wi-Fi.

Download & Launch

Install FloatCam on your Mac or PC. It appears as a tiny icon in your menu bar or system tray.

Same Wi-Fi Network

Make sure your phone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi. FloatCam handles everything automatically.

Scan QR Code

Open the FloatCam dashboard, then point your phone camera at the QR code. No app install required.

Tap "Start Camera"

Allow camera permissions on your phone. Video appears in the floating window instantly.

Customize & Go

Pick your shape, set zoom, snap it to a corner. FloatCam stays always-on-top, ready for your calls.

Architecture

Peer-to-peer.
No middleman.

Video never leaves your local network. The built-in signaling server runs entirely on your machine.

  [ Mobile Browser ]  iOS Safari / Android Chrome
           |
           |  HTTPS + WSS + WebRTC  (Local Wi-Fi / LAN)
           |
  [ Signaling Server ]  Node.js HTTPS + WebSocket :9211
           |
  [ Electron Main Process ]  main.js / IPC Bus
           |
    +------+------+
    |             |
  [ Preview Window ]   [ Control Dashboard ]
  Floating Camera     Menu Bar Popover
